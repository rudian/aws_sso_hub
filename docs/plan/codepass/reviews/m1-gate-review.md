# Milestone 1 gate: design review

Run 2026-11-17 by the orchestrator. 37 screenshots were re-taken in the Vite mock at 1440x900 (the gate list: projects 4, setup 7, identity 3, update 2, settings 2; plus the clone modal, the Projects search while searching and with no match, Health with ToolRows, and the F1-22 Settings rows in three permission states). Fable compared each with its mockup shot in `docs/design/codepass-mockup/shots/`, or with the design text where no shot exists, and read the startup dialog texts (`internal/app/startup.go`, `internal/appguard`) and the notification texts (design 6) in both languages. The owner reviewed the result on a private review page.

**Owner decision (2026-11-17):** D41 as below; everything else as recommended: D7 (Fable leaned yes) is fixed, FU-17e is accepted. 19 items are fixed by **F1-GF**; 31 are accepted as built.

## Owner notes (2026-11-17)

- **D41.** "Codepass cannot extend a sign-in. What it must do: when the sign-in expires, send a notification with a button in it that signs in, like the AWS SSO sign-in." That notice and its "重新登录 / Sign in again" button are F1-22 (merged; checks C22 to C24). Only the hint under "自动续期登录 / Renew sign-in automatically" changes, so it no longer reads as if Codepass extends the session.
- **One portal, several accounts and roles** (the owner pointed at the Accounts page). One SSO portal holds several cards (profiles): the same AWS account with different roles, or different accounts. This is built and stays so: every card of a portal shows under it; "添加账号或角色 / Add account or role" picks more from the portal (`RolePicker` over `ListAccounts` and `ListAccountRoles`, written with `AddProfiles` after a backup of `~/.aws/config`); one sign-in serves every card of the portal; several Identity Center users on one portal are F1-21 (checks C19, C20). The expiry notice names the portal and its AWS account names, each once, and never a role name (design 6), so two roles of one account show one name. New manual check C28.
